\problem  % Problem 59
$C$ - midpoint of chord $AB$.
Through $C$ are drawn $2$ chords, their ends are connected.
Prove, that the resulting chords cut off equal segments on $AB$.

Sol:
Let $AC = CB = a$.
\begin{alignat*}{2}
  p q &= a^2 - x^2  &\qquad  p = \frac{x \sin\beta}{\sin\varphi}  \\
  m n &= a^2 - y^2  &\qquad  q = \frac{x \sin\alpha}{\sin\paren{\varphi + \alpha + \beta}}
\end{alignat*}
\begin{align*}
  p q &= \frac{x^2 \sin\alpha \sin\beta}{\sin\paren{\varphi + \alpha + \beta} \sin\varphi}
       = x^2 A  \\
  m n &= y^2 A
  \implies
  x^2 = \frac{a^2}{A + 1}
      = y^2
\end{align*}
